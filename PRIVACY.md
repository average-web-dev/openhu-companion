# Privacy policy – OpenHU companion app

Last updated: 2026-11-08

The OpenHU companion app ("the app", package `org.openhu.companion`) shows the music library of an OpenHU head unit
in Android Auto and controls its player.

**What the app does with data**

- The app talks only to an OpenHU head unit in your car, over a Bluetooth connection between your phone and that head
  unit. The head unit opens this connection when Android Auto starts. Song titles, artists, album names and covers
  come from the head unit. Your choices (play, pause, a song) go back to it.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. The app has no internet permission, no analytics, no ads
  and no accounts.
- The app keeps no data on the phone beyond what Android needs to run it.

**Permissions**

- *Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect):* needed for the connection to the head unit. It is not used to find or track
  other devices.

**Contact**

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/average-web-dev/openhu-companion/issues.
